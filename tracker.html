WHY 30g PER MEAL ON GLP-1?
Your body needs ~3g of leucine (found in 30g of complete protein) to trigger Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) — the process that preserves and builds muscle. GLP-1 medications cause rapid fullness, so each meal window is critical. Without 30g protein per meal, the weight you lose comes from muscle, not just fat. On GLP-1s, protein percentage must go UP, not down.

How to Rotate Correctly
⟳ THE 4-ZONE ABDOMEN ROTATION
  • Zone 1 — Upper Right abdomen (your right side, below ribs, above navel)
  • Zone 2 — Upper Left abdomen (your left side, below ribs, above navel)
  • Zone 3 — Lower Right abdomen (your right side, below navel, above groin)
  • Zone 4 — Lower Left abdomen (your left side, below navel, above groin)
  • All zones must be at least 2 inches (5cm) from your navel to avoid nerve clusters and the tough tissue around the belly button
  • Rotate: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → repeat. Never use the same zone two days in a row
  • Thigh option (LT/RT): Outer thigh, mid-thigh region is a valid alternative site for some peptides
💉 MULTIPLE PEPTIDES — HOW TO ROTATE
  • Option A (Assign zones): Give each peptide its own zone. BPC-157 always Zone 1–2, TB-500 always Zone 3–4. They never share a zone.
  • Option B (Shared rotation): Rotate all peptides through all 4 zones in sequence. Track each one separately — if BPC used Zone 1 today, TB-500 uses Zone 2 next.
  • Never inject two peptides in the same spot on the same day even if they are mixed in the same syringe
  • Wait at least 1–2 days before reusing any zone regardless of which peptide
