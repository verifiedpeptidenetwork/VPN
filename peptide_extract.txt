🚨 GLP-1 COMPOUND RISKS — READ CAREFULLY
GLP-1 and dual/triple agonist compounds (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide, etc.) carry  serious risks for specific people.
People with personal or family history of  Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma (MTC)  or  MEN 2  must NOT use GLP-1 agents — FDA boxed warning. Absolute contraindication.
Rapid blood sugar reduction from GLP-1 agents has been linked to  worsening of diabetic retinopathy  — a condition that causes permanent blindness. Diabetics with pre-existing retinopathy face serious vision loss risk.
History of  pancreatitis  is a significant contraindication. GLP-1 agents are associated with pancreatic inflammation and life-threatening recurrence risk.
Sourcing peptides outside licensed compounding pharmacies carries serious risks:  unknown purity, incorrect dosing, contamination, no sterility guarantee.  Always verify COAs from independent labs like Janoshik before any research use.

📉 Restart At A Low Dose — Don't Jump To Your Old Number
