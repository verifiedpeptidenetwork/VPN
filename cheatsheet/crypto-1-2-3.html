🚫 Never pay vendors directly. Always pay from or through Trust Wallet.

If you buy from Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, Coinbase and so on and send to your vendor directly, you risk getting your account banned. Always send from your personal wallet, like Trust Wallet.

💳 Payment options and holds

📱 Install Trust Wallet on your phone if you haven't already.

🤔 Which crypto to use, and how to buy it

There are two kinds of crypto:

  • Non-stablecoin: ETH, SOL, TRON, BTC (the price of $1 moves up and down).
  • Stablecoin: USDC or USDT ($1 = $1, but it needs gas to send).
💡 Answer: whichever your vendor takes with the lowest fees. Ask them!

💸 Buy and send crypto

🧪 Always start with a test transaction ($20–30).

🛣️ Stablecoin route: buy 2 things, predictable

  1. Buy USDT/USDC plus gas (SOL, ETH or TRON) from an exchange.
  2. Send both to Trust Wallet.
  3. Send the stablecoin to your vendor from Trust Wallet.

🕐 Holds and fees for buying crypto

🏦 Coinbase / KrakenHold time: can be instant, or up to a week for new users. Fee: 0% for USDC with a bank account on Coinbase.
📱 MoonPay, XO Pay, RampHold time: often just a few hours. Fee: about 4% higher, but they accept Apple Pay.

Most of us use Coinbase to buy USDC, send it to Trust Wallet, and swap it for whatever stablecoin we need to send to the vendor at the time.

⛽ Gas fees and which crypto to use

🛒 What do I need to buy?

Ethereum network (ERC-20)ETH (gas) + USDC / USDT → send to the vendor from Trust Wallet
Solana networkSOL (gas) + USDC / USDT → send to the vendor from Trust Wallet
Tron network (TRC-20)TRON (gas) + USDC / USDT → send to the vendor from Trust Wallet

💰 How much gas?

Solana / Ethereum: usually $0.20 at most per transfer. $10–20 is enough for a year.

TRON costs the most of any crypto to send.

⚙️ How it works

  1. Have the stablecoin (USDT/USDC) in your Trust Wallet. This is your payment amount.
  2. Have the network's own coin (SOL, ETH, etc.) in your wallet. This is your gas money.
  3. When you send, Trust Wallet automatically takes the gas from your wallet.

🧪 Final tip

Don't be scared! Before sending crypto, always do a small $5 test transaction and have your vendor confirm they received it.
