RESEARCH Do Peptides Cause Cancer? The Real Answer Is Far More Complicated Than You Think ▼

"Peptides cause cancer" is one of the most repeated fears — and one of the least supported by actual biology. The truth is a genuine tug-of-war between competing mechanisms.

⚠ ANGIOGENESIS CONCERN
BPC-157 promotes blood vessel formation — same process tumors use. BUT: in healthy individuals with no existing tumor, no evidence this initiates cancer.
🛡️ THYMIC PROTECTION
Thymosin Alpha-1 & TB-500 support thymus → T-cells that target tumors. Thymic peptides may REDUCE cancer risk in immunosenescent populations.
🔫 IMMUNOSURVEILLANCE
Peptides enhancing NK cell/T-cell activity improve detection and destruction of pre-cancerous cells.
🔥 INFLAMMATION → CANCER
Chronic inflammation is one of the most established cancer drivers. Anti-inflammatory peptides (BPC-157, KPV) may be PROTECTIVE.
⚖️ THE ACTUAL ANSWER

Active cancer diagnosis: consult oncologist before any growth-promoting peptides. Healthy individual with no known malignancy: the fear is largely unsupported by evidence. Context, dose, and individual biology are everything. Always verify with bloodwork.
