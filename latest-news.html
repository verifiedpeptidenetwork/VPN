🦠 NEW KPV Has a Self-Targeting Effect — Gut Healing Peptide That Finds Inflammation ▼
Nick Norwitz MD PhD  ·  Jul 05, 2026  ·  staycuriousmetabolism.substack.com ↗
🔒 Article behind paywall — follow him on Substack to read more
KPV (Lysine-Proline-Valine) is an anti-inflammatory tripeptide derived from alpha-MSH. What makes it remarkable is a self-targeting mechanism — inflamed gut tissue upregulates PEPT1 transporters, and KPV is preferentially absorbed through those same transporters. More inflammation = more PEPT1 = more KPV uptake exactly where it's needed. It was removed from the FDA Category 2 prohibited list as of May 2026.

💈 NEW PP405 — Hair Follicle Stem Cell Reactivator Moving Toward Late-Stage Trials ▼
Dermatology Times  ·  2026  ·  Pelage Pharmaceuticals ↗
PP405 is an investigational topical small-molecule drug developed by Pelage Pharmaceuticals to treat hair loss by reactivating dormant hair follicle stem cells. Not yet FDA-approved or commercially available as a prescription medication.
🔬 HOW PP405 WORKS
Cellular mechanism: Inhibits the mitochondrial pyruvate carrier (MPC) to trigger a metabolic shift in resting hair follicles.
Growth stimulation: Signals dormant stem cells to re-enter the proliferative growth phase (anagen).
Target delivery: Formulated as a topical treatment designed to avoid systemic absorption — works locally at the follicle level.
⚠ NOTE: Unrelated over-the-counter scalp serums using the "PP405" name have appeared online (including Amazon). These are not the same compound as the Pelage Pharmaceuticals investigational drug. The pharmaceutical PP405 is not commercially available.
